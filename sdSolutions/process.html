Five steps. Same order, every time.

We have run this with over a thousand people. It works because it is sequential: each step feeds the next, instead of resume, applications and interview prep all happening at once.

1

Audit

A 30-minute call. We tell you what's working in your search and what isn't.

2

Rebuild

Your new resume in three business days, with two rounds of edits.

3

Aim

Two recorded mock interviews and a written scorecard before the real one.

What happens at each step

1

Audit

We start with a 30-minute call about your resume, LinkedIn profile and the roles you want. You leave with a plain-language read on what is working, what is not, and which of our services will actually help.

Takes 30 minutes. Notes the same day.
2

Rebuild

We rebuild your resume for the roles you are targeting, with formatting that passes applicant-tracking filters and wording a recruiter will read past the first six seconds.

Ready in 3 business days, with 2 rounds of edits.
3

Aim

We match your new profile to the platforms where you have the best odds, from the big boards to niche communities and direct recruiter networks, so you stop spreading effort across places that never reply.

Takes 2 business days. You get a ranked list.
4

Apply

Now the applying starts: five to ten targeted roles a week, walked through with you or sent for you, depending on your plan. Every application is tailored. Nothing is copy-pasted.

Weekly rhythm. Every reply is logged.
5

Rehearse

Once calls start landing, we run two full recorded mock interviews against questions for your role, then send a written scorecard on structure, clarity and delivery.

Two sessions, plus a scorecard.
