We got tired of watching good people get ignored.

SD Solutions is a career consultancy built on one idea: a better resume is wasted if nobody helps you use it. So we stay with you from the first edit to the last interview round.

Built from the recruiter's side of the table.

Strong candidates kept losing. Not because they lacked skills, but because their resume never got past the first screen, or they walked into an interview not knowing what to expect.

So we built a service that closes that gap: people who have reviewed resumes and run interview loops, working through one clear process that anyone can follow.
